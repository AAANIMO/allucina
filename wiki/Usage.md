# Usage

## Modes

- **Projection** (on launch): black screen, no UI. Move the mouse → the floating
  buttons appear bottom-right (Edit, Invert, Auto, **?**, Exit), or press `E`.
- **Edit**: top toolbar, properties panel on the right, file bar bottom-left and
  the **animosity ・ allucina** title top-left. Press **Done ✓** or `E` to go back
  to projecting: everything disappears, only the composition remains.

## Splash

On open you see the animosity logo, then **allucina**, **an animosity project**
and the buttons: **start allucina »** (enter), **go to website**, **buy me a
coffee** and **help ? guide »** (opens this wiki). The floating **Exit** button
brings you back here anytime.

## Gestures (Apple trackpad)

- **Pinch** → zoom centered on the cursor, infinite.
- **Two-finger scroll** → horizontal/vertical pan, infinite.
- With a mouse: **spacebar + drag** to pan.

## Shapes & objects

- Toolbar → Rectangle, Circle, Ellipse, Triangle, Bar, **Cross**.
- **Vertices**: click to drop points, `Enter`/double-click closes, `Esc` cancels.
  Double-click a polygon to edit its vertices.
- **Text** (blackletter: Unifraktur Maguntia/Cook, Pirata One, Grenze Gotisch):
  double-click to edit inline; pick font and size in the panel.
- **Import SVG** / **Import image** (SVGs are turned white for projection).
- With an object selected: drag to move, **corner** handles to scale, **side**
  handles to squash, top handle to **rotate**.

## Invert colors

**◑ Invert** (toolbar, floating button, or `I`) inverts colors across the
**whole screen**: black↔white. Editor panels stay readable. The state is saved
with autosave/export.

**Auto-invert** (**⏱ Auto** or `Shift+I`) inverts on a regular interval — set the
seconds (0.1–10) in the field next to it. Useful as a strobe. Changing the
interval while active restarts it with the new value.

## Clear & Exit

- **🗑 Clear** (toolbar) empties the canvas — a confirmation lets you **export
  first** or clear without saving.
- **Exit** (floating) returns to the splash screen.

## Files

- **Autosave**: reopen and everything is as you left it.
- **Export / Import** a `.json` project.
- **New** empties the canvas.
